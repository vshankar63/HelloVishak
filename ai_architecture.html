Layer 4 — Synthesis Core
Phase 2 · Strategic
⚡
Product Ops Intelligence Core
The connective layer. Aggregates outputs from all five agents into a unified view. Identifies cross-agent patterns — a feature flagged by the Launch Readiness Agent that also shows negative signal in the Feedback Loop Agent. Surfaces conflicts and priorities to the Product Ops lead. Only viable once Layers 1, 2, and 3 are stable and producing reliable signal.
Cross-agent reasoning Conflict detection Weekly digest
From Synthesis Agent
Friction themes ranked by frequency and severity. Used to prioritise which process to fix next and where structural change is most needed.
From Launch and Feedback Agents
Correlation between readiness gaps and post-launch customer confusion. Informs checklist refinement and engagement model updates over time.
From All Agents
Weekly health report: what is stuck, what is flowing, where R&D and GTM are misaligned. Single source of truth for Hugo and Product Directors.
